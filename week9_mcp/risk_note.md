1. WHO WROTE IT: the claims-platform team's artifact, vendored unmodified into `week9_mcp/claims_thirdparty/` — not our code, reviewed by us only for protocol compatibility, not security.
2. WHAT IT CAN REACH: every claim in the claims DB it ships with, by any `CLM-YYYY-nnnnn` (status, claimant, policy, adjuster-note history) — and because it runs on our agent's stdio pipe inside the same process, it shares our process's permissions and sees our server's wire traffic.
3. WHAT IT LOGS: nothing itself (its stdout is reserved for JSON-RPC), but our host logs every `tools/call` it serves — claim numbers and full note text — into `wire/` and `traces/`, so claimant PII exits through our logs regardless.
4. A STOLEN TOKEN COULD: call `claim_adjuster_notes` for an unbounded, un-authorized list of claim numbers — the artifact has no rate limit, no per-claim access check and no scope expiry, exposing claimant names + damage details for every open claim.
5. VERDICT: don't ship as-is — keep it only behind the gateway's scoped token that denies `claim_adjuster_notes` (bonus) and make the vendor sign their artifact + audit logs; fine for the read-only staging lookup we ran.
