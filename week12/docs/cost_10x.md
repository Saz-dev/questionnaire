## First thing that breaks

**The provider tokens-per-minute limit, if the model is on the hot path.** At the peak-hour rate of 7.5 FNOL/min the LLM path needs ~18,300 TPM against an 8,000 TPM limit (2.3x over); the limit is crossed at ~3.3 FNOL/min. Dollar cost for the whole 18,000-FNOL week is ~$9, so it is not the cost. The default no-model path has ~5,700x compute headroom, so it does not break; what grows is the adjuster referral queue (every claim ends in a referral by design).

Same method as `week11/tenx.md` (TPM breaks first, not cost or latency), recomputed at hailstorm volume instead of 1 claim/min. The 60%-in-48h and 2x-peak-hour shape are assumptions I chose; change them in `cost.py` and re-run.
